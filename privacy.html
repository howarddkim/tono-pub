Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Tono Translate is a translation and language-learning app. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your translations, lessons and feedback requests pass through our servers to an AI provider and are not stored there; your library lives on your device; we don't run ads, we don't track you across apps, and we never sell data.

What we collect

Account. Tono Translate does not ask you to register. On first launch the app creates an anonymous identifier for your device so your free allowance and your subscription can be recognized; it contains no name, email or Apple ID. It is shared only with the companies that run the app for us, listed under Who receives it, below, and RevenueCat uses it as your subscriber number. Your subscription itself belongs to your Apple ID, not to us.

Proof of a genuine app. To keep the free allowance from being abused, the app asks Apple's App Attest service to confirm that it is a genuine copy of Tono Translate on a real device. We keep the key identifier Apple returns with your anonymous identifier. It identifies the app installation, not you.

Content you submit for translation, feedback or lessons. Phrases you type, context you provide, photos you take of text, and transcriptions of speech are sent to our servers and forwarded to our AI provider (currently Google's Gemini API) to produce the translation. When you ask for feedback on a sentence you wrote, that sentence is sent the same way, and so is the material a weekly lesson is written from (listed under Your library, below). We do not store this content on our servers — it passes through to produce your answer, and nothing we keep contains what you translated or submitted.

Our AI provider processes it under its own terms, including Google's API data-use policy. We use Google's paid API tier, under which submitted content is not used to train or improve Google's models. If we ever change providers or tiers in a way that affects this, we will update this policy.

Where processing happens. Our servers and our AI provider operate in the United States and other countries, so content you submit for translation, feedback or lessons is processed outside your own country, including outside the EU/EEA and the UK.

Speech. Speech is converted to text by Apple's speech recognition, on your device where supported. On some devices Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your audio. Only the resulting text is sent for translation, and only when you ask for one.

Your library. Translation history, scripts, and starred phrases are stored locally on your device, not on our servers. Weekly lessons, practice cards and your practice history, and the phrases shown in the Home Screen widget are also stored only on your device. A weekly lesson is generated the same way as a translation: the week's phrases and script titles, last week's lesson summary, and the goal and level you set are sent through our servers to our AI provider to write the lesson, and are not stored there. If you turn on conversation saving in Talk (off by default), full transcripts — including the other person's words — are also kept on your device only, with a visible indicator while saving is on, until you delete them.

Usage counts. We record how many translations are made per day against your device's anonymous identifier — this enforces the free allowance and helps us understand load. Counts only; not the content. We also count, per day, how many people reach each step of the app's first-time setup — a tally of numbers with no identifier attached, so it cannot be connected to you.

Subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, which processes your Apple transaction identifiers under its own privacy policy. When you tap to subscribe, the app also notes which screen you were on (for example, the offer at the end of first-time setup, or the Practice tab), and RevenueCat keeps that note with your subscription, under your device's anonymous identifier. We use it only to see which parts of the app lead people to subscribe.

Server logs. Like any web service, our server host keeps standard request logs, including IP addresses, for a few days, for security and to prevent abuse (for example, limiting how many new identifiers one network can create in an hour).

Who receives it, and where

A few companies run parts of Tono Translate for us. Each receives only what its job needs, over an encrypted connection, when you use the feature:

  • Google LLC (United States), the Gemini API: the text, context and photos you submit, to write your answer. Not kept by us, and not used to train Google's models.
  • Supabase, Inc. (United States), our server host: your anonymous identifier, your counts and subscription status, the App Attest key identifier and request logs. What you submit passes through it on its way to Google.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States), subscription management: your anonymous identifier, your Apple transaction identifiers, and the screen you subscribed from.
  • Apple, for purchases, App Attest, and speech recognition when it isn't done on your device, under Apple's privacy policy.

To keep what you translate on your iPhone, turn on Settings → Translate on this device: Translate, Talk, Show Mode and face-to-face then translate on the device, with the words only and none of the explanations. Photos, Polish, Ask about this, Scripts, weekly lessons, the share sheet and Siri still use our servers.

Why we use it

We use what you submit to give you the answer you asked for, and your identifier, counts and subscription status to provide the service you chose (in EU and UK terms, to perform our contract with you). We use the App Attest check, the request logs, the setup tally and the screen you subscribed from in our legitimate interest in preventing abuse, keeping the service running and understanding what works. The website's analytics cookie in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and the app's notifications, depend on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

How long we keep it

What you submit is not kept: it exists on our servers only while your answer is being written. Your anonymous identifier, with its counts, subscription status and App Attest key identifier, is kept while you use the app. When it has gone 12 months without use or a subscription, we delete it automatically, and you can ask us to delete it sooner. Request logs are kept for a few days. The setup tally is kept as totals only. RevenueCat and Apple keep subscription records under their own policies.

Website analytics

Our marketing website (tonotranslate.com, including this page) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people find useful. It records page views, the site you came from, approximate location derived from a truncated IP address, and general device and browser type, and it sets a cookie in your browser. This is about website visitors, not app users: it is not connected to your Tono Translate account, and the app itself contains no analytics or tracking SDKs.

If you're in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, that cookie is not set unless you accept it — we ask before anything is stored, and declining is one click. You can change your answer at any time with Cookie settings, at the bottom of every page. We do not use advertising cookies anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking of you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No sale of any data, ever.

A note on other people

Tono Translate's conversation features can transcribe what someone speaking another language says to you. That transcription is processed the same way as anything else you translate — Apple's speech recognition as described above, text sent for translation, not stored by us. If you use these features in a conversation, it's your responsibility to tell the other person, and to get their consent where the law requires it. Please be considerate about using them where recording assistance is inappropriate.

Deleting your data

Your library — every phrase, script and transcript — exists only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. The widget keeps a copy of up to 30 phrases on your device. After you erase your library, it's cleared the next time you open the app. If you delete the app, iOS may keep this copy until the device is erased. Inside the app, Settings → Erase my library removes the same content without uninstalling, and Reset app returns settings to first launch. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, your library is included in that backup. We have no access to it.

What stays on our servers is your anonymous identifier with your translation counts, subscription status and App Attest key identifier. It carries no name, no email address and none of what you translated. To have it deleted, use Settings → Delete my server data, which emails us that identifier, or write to hello@tonotranslate.com and we'll help you find it. We delete it within a month. Afterwards the app starts with a new identifier; if you subscribe, restore your subscription from the subscription screen. Canceling a subscription is separate and handled through your Apple account settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU, the UK, Brazil and Korea, you have the right to ask what we hold about you, and to have it corrected, deleted or restricted, to object to how we use it, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw your consent. Send your request to hello@tonotranslate.com with your device's identifier (Settings → Delete my server data includes it), and we'll answer within a month. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Korea, that is the Personal Information Protection Commission (pipc.go.kr) or the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, phone 118).

Children

Tono Translate is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the app, write to us and we'll delete what our server holds for that device.
