Drills

Drills from your own phrases

Not a word list: the sentences you actually needed. Say one from memory, work out what a message says, or pick which version fits which person. A few minutes at a time, and it works offline.

Weekly lesson

A short lesson, written from your week

Once a week, Tono Translate reads what you looked up and writes a short lesson on the level of formality you’re building. It asks you for phrases from memory, brings back earlier ones, and gives gentle feedback on a sentence of your own. Kept on your phone, never on our servers.

Home Screen widget

A phrase a day on your Home Screen

Add the Tono Translate widget and each day it brings back one phrase you looked up, with the meaning hidden. Try to remember it, then tap Show the meaning. Tap the card to open the phrase in the app. With Tono Translate Pro.

Why it matters

Right words,
wrong level.

Get a word wrong and someone corrects you. Get the level of formality wrong and nobody says anything — and in Korean, Japanese or Spanish, the level is the message.

To your manager · deferential
괜찮습니다
Reassuring and respectful. The formal ending holds exactly the distance the moment asks for.
To a friend, a little annoyed · casual
아, 됐어
“Forget it.” The same three English words — and a completely different relationship afterwards.
